How to Choose the Right
Running Shoe — Buyer's Guide

With so many options available — both Indian and international — it's easy to get overwhelmed. Use these four pillars to narrow down the perfect shoe for your needs.

Foot Type — Know Yours First

🦶
Flat Feet (Low Arch)
Your foot rolls inward when you run — called overpronation. You need a stability or motion-control shoe. Best pick: Jenzya React Flex.
✅
Neutral Arch
The most common foot type. Almost any shoe works well. Focus on cushioning and weight. Best picks: KX Pro, Zoom Turbo, Phantom Elite.
⬆️
High Arch
Your foot supinates (rolls outward). You need maximum cushioning and a flexible sole. Best pick: Jenzya Phantom Elite or Cloud Walk.
💡
Wet Test at Home
Wet your foot, step on newspaper, and look at the imprint. Full imprint = flat. Half moon gap = neutral. Thin outer band = high arch.

Cushioning Type

EVA (Ethylene Vinyl Acetate) is the standard — lightweight, affordable, and well-cushioned. Found in the KX Pro and React Flex. React Foam adds energy return — it bounces back, propelling you forward. Found in the Zoom Turbo. Dual-density stack combines soft heel cushioning with a firmer forefoot plate for long-distance comfort — the Phantom Elite uses this approach.

Terrain Type

Indian roads are not all created equal. For smooth tarmac roads, go with a road running shoe like the KX Pro with its rubber outsole. For parks and uneven paths, look for multi-lug outsoles like the Trail Crusher. For gym treadmills, almost any shoe works — but a lighter model like the Speedlite gives a better feel.

How to Care for Your
Running Shoes

A quality pair of running shoes can last 600–800 km — but only if you maintain them. India's monsoons, heat, and dusty roads can cut that life in half if you're not careful.

🚿
Clean After Every Run
Use a soft brush and mild soap. Never machine wash — it breaks down EVA foam and glue bonds.
☀️
Air Dry in Shade
Never dry in direct sunlight or with a hairdryer. Heat degrades foam. Stuff with newspaper to hold shape.
🔄
Rotate Two Pairs
Foam needs 24–48 hrs to decompress. Alternating two shoes doubles the lifespan of each.
🧦
Wear Running Socks
Moisture-wicking socks reduce sweat absorption into the insole, preventing odour and foam breakdown.
📦
Store Properly
Keep in a cool, dry place out of direct sunlight. Don't crush them under other items in your bag.
📅
Replace at 600 km
Track your mileage. When the midsole feels compressed or grip fades, it's time for a new pair.
